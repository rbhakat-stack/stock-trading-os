4. DEFINITIVE SWING TERMINOLOGY
============================================================

Use these definitions consistently.

SWING HIGH

A meaningful local peak:

price rises
-> reaches peak
-> reverses downward

SWING LOW

A meaningful local trough:

price falls
-> reaches trough
-> reverses upward

UPSWING

One meaningful movement from:

swing low -> swing high

DOWNSWING

One meaningful movement from:

swing high -> swing low

Critical rule:

Swing highs/lows are POINTS.

Upswings/downswings are MOVEMENTS.

Do not classify every candle high/low as a meaningful swing.

============================================================

SIDEWAYS / RANGE:

No sustained HH+HL or LH+LL structure.

Avoid ambiguous terminology such as:

downswing

============================================================
7. DEFINITIVE TREND-CONFIRMATION LOGIC
============================================================

Use this exact educational framework.

BULLISH SEQUENCE

Begin:

Swing High 1 = SH1
Swing Low 1 = SL1

Price subsequently breaks above SH1.

That creates:

Higher High = HH
Bullish Break of Structure = Bullish BOS

BUT:

Do NOT declare the uptrend confirmed at the instant SH1 breaks because the next swing low is not yet known.

Wait for pullback.

If subsequent pullback holds above SL1 and forms:

Higher Low = HL

Then:

HH + HL = confirmed uptrend structure.

Sequence:

SH1
-> SL1
-> break above SH1
-> HH / Bullish BOS
-> pullback
-> HL above SL1
-> CONFIRMED UPTREND

BEARISH MIRROR IMAGE

SL1
-> SH1
-> break below SL1
-> LL / Bearish BOS
-> rebound
-> LH below SH1
-> CONFIRMED DOWNTREND

This distinction must remain explicit in the software.

9. CHANGE OF CHARACTER — CHOCH
============================================================

Use this definition:

CHOCH =
first meaningful structural break AGAINST an established trend.

Established uptrend:

break below latest important HL
=
Bearish CHOCH

Established downtrend:

break above latest important LH
=
Bullish CHOCH

CHOCH is:

EARLY WARNING

CHOCH is NOT:

CONFIRMED REVERSAL

For bearish reversal progression monitor:

-> bearish CHOCH
-> first LL
-> rebound
-> LH
-> continuation to new LL
-> stronger bearish confirmation

For bullish reversal use mirror logic.

The UI should explicitly display:

Terminology may differ between trading communities, therefore the underlying HH/HL/LH/LL structure must always be visible.

16. FAILED BREAKOUT VS FAKEOUT
============================================================

FAILED BREAKOUT:

price moves beyond level but eventually loses acceptance and returns into prior range.

FAKEOUT:

very brief break followed by rapid rejection.

BREAKOUT THAT LATER FAILED:

price genuinely establishes acceptance for meaningful time/distance and only later loses the level.

Track these separately because their statistics may differ substantially.

74. FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Build the platform around this hierarchy:

1. Is the market data trustworthy?

2. What is the market doing?

3. What is the market regime?

4. Is there a recognized, validated setup?

5. Where is the structural invalidation?

6. What is the maximum acceptable loss?

7. Is the expected reward sufficient?

8. Does historical evidence support the setup in this regime?

9. Does portfolio risk permit the trade?

10. Is execution quality acceptable?

11. Are event/regulatory/system risks acceptable?

12. TRADE, WAIT, or NO TRADE.

The system must never reverse this hierarchy by starting with:

"What can I buy right now?"

The core operating philosophy is:

DESCRIBE -> CLASSIFY -> VALIDATE -> SIZE -> EXECUTE -> REVIEW -> LEARN.

The purpose of the platform is not maximum trading activity.

It is maximum decision quality per unit of risk.
